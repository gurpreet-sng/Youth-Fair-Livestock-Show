Our Mission

Growing
Tomorrow's
Farmers

The Alachua County Youth Fair & Livestock Show Association is a non-profit organization that conducts and operates public fairs pertaining to agricultural matters — including exhibitions of livestock, poultry, crafts, youth projects, and farm products.

The fair is an educational platform for the youth in Alachua County who are interested in agriculture. Youth from 4-H and FFA programs across the county come together to showcase months of hard work and dedication.
